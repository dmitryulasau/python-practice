Chocolate Fudge

Description

And as we get near the end of this parade of goodies – treats that are fun to make, and perfect to give – I needed to include an easy chocolate fudge recipe.

Make this fudge anytime you need a quick treat to set out at a party or to gift. Your friends and family will thank you!

Ingredients

  • 3 cups chocolate chips
  • 14 ounces sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Steps

  1. Line an 8 inch by 8 inch baking dish.
  2. In a medium saucepan, combine the chocolate chips and sweetened condensed milk.
  3. Place the pan over medium low heat. Stir consistently until the chocolate is partially melted. Stir in the vanilla extract.
  4. Continue stirring until the chocolate is fully melted and the mixture is smooth and starts to turn shiny.
